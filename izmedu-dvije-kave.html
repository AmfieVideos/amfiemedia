Između Dvije Kave

Production management and visual identity for a student-led podcast.

Project Overview

Students wanted a podcast to talk about student life, campus culture, and university experiences with occasional guests. They wanted a casual, laid-back atmosphere, free from political or corporate themes.


While the students had the initial concept, they needed a technical partner to execute it. That is where I came in to set up the entire production workflow and advertising system.

Project Execution

First, I established the podcast style and educated the team on the technical fundamentals of podcasting, studio production, and social media advertising.


For the visual identity, the team requested two cups of coffee, a microphone, and the University North red color. I designed the primary logo, social media flyers, posters, and stickers to launch the brand. Additionally, I managed the technical setup of the studio, ensuring the audio and video quality matched the casual, high-quality tone they envisioned.
