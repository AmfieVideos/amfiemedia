Umbral Scavenger

A dark-themed scavenger hunt game.

Try the game out

Concept

Umbral Scavenger is a dark-themed scavenger hunt game. Players must navigate through a mysterious world to find hidden items and solve puzzles.


Three generations after the sky went dark, natural sunlight is gone—condensed into physical, luminescent mineral veins known as Light-Marrow pulsing deep within the earth. As an Umbral Scavenger, you are a solitary keeper equipped with specialized optical lenses, harvesting ampoules, and marrow-fueled gear venturing into treacherous subterranean depths and freezing mountain crags.

Companions

Umbral Scavenger features a cast of unique characters who serve as companions to the player. Each companion has their own personality and abilities that can be utilized to solve puzzles and overcome challenges.
