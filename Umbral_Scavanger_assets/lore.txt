World Lore: The Sundered Crust & The Umbral Frontier
A comprehensive compendium documenting the history, ecology, mechanics, ancient civilizations, settlements, and bestiary of the light-starved world.
1. World Overview & The Cataclysm
Three generations ago, the sky fractured and collapsed inward during a cataclysmic extraction overload, permanently extinguishing natural orbital sunlight. The surface was left trapped in perpetual, freezing twilight, while true radiant illumination collapsed into the deep bedrock of the planet.
In this era, illumination is not merely electromagnetic radiation; it exists as dense, physical states of luminescent fluid and mineral distillates known collectively as Light-Marrow. Without containment and sustained heat, these distillates calcify into brittle glass. Where light completely recedes, the Living Gloom settles—an inverted, high-pressure atmospheric vapor that crushes lung capacity, petrifies organic material, and suffocates unprotected life.

	3. Ancient Civilizations: The Zenith Order
Before the cataclysm, the Zenith Order ruled as an empire of scholar-engineers and astronomers. They mastered light physics, building cyclopean astrolabes, mountain observatories, and deep subterranean refiners to extract and condense orbital energy directly into the planetary crust.
* The Sunken Scriptorium: An ancient gothic archive and observatory buried beneath the Ashfang Ridge, housing calcified astronomer codices, silver notched coordinate cylinders, and astrolabe daises.
* The Glacial Core Chamber: A sacred cathedral beneath the northern ice shelf containing the Resonance Heart—a suspended, wagon-sized crystal geode used to regulate planetary seismic frequencies.
* Bio-Mechanical Titans: 40-foot monolithic sentinels like the Obsidian Patriarch, sculpted from volcanic obsidian glass and cast iron, built to safeguard the True Sun-Well and command radial ranks of clockwork automata.

* Frost-Tempered Violet Silica Armor: Concave breastplates that channel radiant marrow heat inward to vitals while deflecting freezing drafts and blunt impacts.
